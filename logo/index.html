HOUSE OF CHAOS

Three chrome logo concepts. Each one comes as a chrome render (transparent PNG) and as flat vector marks for print, merch and stamps.

01X · Extra

The first wordmark turned all the way up: wilder tumble, a dark 3D extrusion, a Y2K double chrome outline and glints.

01 · Wordmark

HOUSE stays in line, CHAOS falls apart. Both words have five letters, so they stack into a block.

01B · Sliced

Same stacked block, but CHAOS is cut into strips that slide sideways, like a glitch or a skipping record.

01C · One line

HOUSE ✷ CHAOS. A chaos star replaces “OF”. Made for banners, headers and the bottom of flyers.

01D · Tower

Tall condensed poster type with “OF” running vertically and CHAOS tumbling. Strong on stories and posters.

01E · Monogram

H ✷ C with the full name underneath. Compact enough for a profile picture, stamp or merch chest print.

02 · Seal

Label stamp with an eight-arrow chaos star. Works as a profile picture, vinyl centre label or wristband.

03 · Liquid

Melted lowercase "chaos" with drips, the loose and playful side of the brand.
